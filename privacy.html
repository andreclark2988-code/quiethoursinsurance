Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026. This policy explains how Quiet Hours Insurance ("we," "us") collects, uses and protects your information.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email and ZIP code.
  • What you tell us about the coverage you're looking for.
  • Appointment details and the messages you send us.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, schedule appointments and prepare insurance options for you.
  • To send you the text messages and emails you agreed to receive.
  • To submit applications you ask us to submit to insurance carriers.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. We share information with insurance carriers only to quote or apply for coverage you request, and with service providers that help us run our business, who must keep it confidential.

Your choices

You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP, and unsubscribe from emails with the link in any email. To see, correct or delete your information, email andre@quiethoursinsurance.com.
